How do I add clothes?

In Wardrobe, tap the + card, then take a photo or choose one from your library. Mira removes the background on your iPhone. Pick a category and save. Clothes laid flat or hung up, on a plain background, in good light, work best.

How does try-on work?

Make an outfit, open it and tap Try It On. Add a full-body photo of yourself, standing, in good light. Mira asks before your photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI, which creates a picture of you wearing the outfit in 10 to 30 seconds. It shows the look, not the fit. Try-on is for people 18 and over. Only use a photo of yourself.

How many try-ons can I make?

Three a day on each iPhone. They come back every day at midnight UTC. A picture that couldn't be made doesn't count.

A try-on didn't work

Check your internet connection and try again. If Mira says a picture couldn't be made from this photo, try a clearer full-body photo of yourself, or another outfit: Google's safety checks turn down some photos. On very busy days, try-on can reach its limit for everyone; it's back the next day.

A try-on picture is offensive or wrong

Open the picture and tap Report (the flag at the top). Mira deletes the picture and opens an email to us: tell us what was wrong and we'll look into it. Your photo isn't sent.

Who can use Mira?

Mira is for people aged 18 and over.

How do I delete my photo?

Tap the gear at the top of Wardrobe or Outfits, then Your Photo, then Delete Photo. This also deletes every try-on picture made with it.

How do I delete clothes or outfits?

Press and hold a piece in Wardrobe and drag it onto the trash button. To delete several at once, tap Select. You can also open a piece and tap Delete Item. To delete an outfit, open it, tap the ••• button, then Delete Outfit.

Is my wardrobe backed up?

Your wardrobe is stored only on your iPhone, with no account. It's included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup, and comes back when you restore that backup.
