How it works

Your wardrobe, in three steps

01

Photograph your clothes

Snap each piece. Mira removes the background right on your iPhone and keeps everything tidy by tops, bottoms, dresses, shoes and more.

02

Mix them into outfits

Place your pieces on a canvas, move, resize and layer them, and save the looks you love.

03

See them on you

Add one full-body photo of yourself, and try any outfit on it with AI. Know what works before you put it on.
