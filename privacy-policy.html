Mira Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Your wardrobe stays on your iPhone

Mira has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The photos of your clothes, their categories, your outfits, your photo for try-on and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it unless you use try-on, described below.

Camera and photos

Mira uses the camera, or the photos you choose from your library, only when you add a piece of clothing or your photo for try-on. Photos you don't choose are never read. Backgrounds are removed on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in tools.

Try-on

Try-on is optional, and Mira asks before your photo is first sent. When you tap Try It On, Mira sends your photo and the pictures of that outfit's clothes to Mira's try-on server, which passes them to Google's Gemini AI, through Google Cloud's Vertex AI, to create the picture. The picture comes back to your iPhone and is saved only there.

Mira's server doesn't store your photo, the clothes or the picture. It holds them only while the picture is being made.

Google doesn't use them to train its AI. Google may keep temporary copies to speed up its service, and deletes them within 24 hours. If Google's automated safety checks flag a request as possible misuse, Google may keep it for up to 90 days, and authorised Google staff may review it, only to check for misuse. Google may handle requests in any of its locations worldwide.

Only use a photo of yourself.

If a picture is offensive or wrong, tap Report on it. Mira deletes the picture and opens an email to us. The email holds only what you write, and we use it only to reply and to improve try-on.

What Mira's server keeps

So that only Mira can use try-on, and each iPhone gets a limited number of pictures a day, the server keeps a record for each iPhone that has used try-on: a random key made on your iPhone by Apple's App Attest, and how many pictures it made on which day. The record holds no name, email, photo or anything else about you, and we can't tell whose iPhone it belongs to.

Google Cloud, which runs the server, keeps standard request logs for up to 30 days, for security and to keep the service running. They include the time, your IP address and the app and iOS versions, but no photos.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include Mira's data, under Apple's terms for backups.

Deleting your data

You can delete any piece or outfit in the app. Settings > Your Photo > Delete Photo removes your photo and every try-on picture made with it. Deleting Mira from your iPhone removes everything it stored there.

You can stop sending photos at any time: delete your photo, and Mira sends nothing until you add a new one and agree again.

The record on Mira's server can't be traced back to you, so we can't find yours to delete on request. It holds nothing about you, and is no longer used once you delete Mira.

Age

Mira is for people aged 18 and over. Try-on is only for photos of yourself.

Changes to this policy

If Mira changes what it sends off your iPhone, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and Mira will ask you before anything new is sent.
